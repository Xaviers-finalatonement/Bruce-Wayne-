**Evidence verdict: UNKNOWN.** A 6-month home-use study found a bacterial film (biofilm) builds up on towels. The same study did not measure anyone's skin or infections, and did not test disposable towels. Dermatology guidance supports not sharing towels and patting skin dry with a clean one.

Spoken hook: "Your towel grows a film of bacteria." (7 words.) Thread: does the film form, where does it come from, does it matter for skin? Ending answers it: "The film is real. Harm to your skin? Unproven."
