## Notes

- The brief asked what studies show about microbes living on towels. Those studies (e.g. towel biofilm and bacterial-count papers) sit on PubMed/PMC, which in this session returned a bot challenge (reCAPTCHA / proof-of-work) that we did not bypass; Nature/Springer hosts were also blocked. So **no count of microbes on towels is said or shown** in the film. The title promise ("what's living on your bath towel") is answered only qualitatively: some germs and fungi can travel on towels.
- Search snippets pointed to (not opened, so OPEN and NOT used in the script): "Biofilm Compositions and Bacterial Diversity on Kitchen Towels in Daily Use" (PMC11767729); "Analysis of biofilm and bacterial communities in the towel environment with daily use" (PMC10172380).
- No product or brand is named or shown; paper towels in the visuals are plain and unbranded.

Open items: (1) PMC11767729 and PMC10172380 not opened (OPEN, unused). With them opened, a line on typical bacterial counts could replace beat 5. Everything spoken in the script is OK.
