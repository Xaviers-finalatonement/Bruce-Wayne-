### Facts and ethics

**OK as shown:**
- **Hook and opening:** "49 women tested it" (C1), "CLAIM: more water → hydrated skin" (C0), "LINE 1 · 49 healthy women" (C1, C2).
- **Method:** "4 weeks" (C2), "no comparison group" with the dashed "?" slot (C3), "over half of their water: food, soup, juice" (C13), "skin hydration probe" and "face · arm · hand · leg" (C4).
- **Results:** 54 → 76 in "forehead reading · probe units". This matches the C6 row verbatim, and the bars are zero-based (the height ratio is about 1.4). "change not clear": the group-2 bars have no numbers, and the rise is shown dashed with "?" (C7). "water loss through skin: no relevant change" plus "illustration, not to scale" (C8).
- **Review and verdict:** "one small study" (C3), "2018 review · 5 studies" and WEAK (C9), "noticeable? unclear" (C10), RECEIPT TOTAL UNKNOWN and "proven? not yet" (C11).
- **Cover:** the headline question, the Skin Receipts pill, RECEIPT TOTAL with UNKNOWN, and the disclaimer.
- **YouTube copy:** the description is correctly hedged ("about 2 liters", "readings rose", "no comparison group").

**What the film does not imply:**
- The 28-box calendar is 4 × 7, and the glasses count is 8 (`scene.js`). Neither implies an amount beyond the study's.
- No graphic implies a control group. Both groups are shown start → week 4, and both received the extra water.
- **Disclaimer:** "Education, not medical advice." is on screen in every sampled frame, on the end card and on the cover.
- **Clean:** no brands, products, dosing or diagnosis.

**OPEN (gating). On-screen text whose hedge does not match its claims row:**
1. **End card (66.8–70.3):** "extra **water raised** skin hydration readings". This is causal, and the study had no comparison group (C3). The film's own point is that we can't say the water did it. `platform.json` gets it right: "readings rose".
2. **Summary strip (57–62):** "**might help** · mostly if you drink little". No row says "help". C11 is "might nudge … hydration **readings**", and "help" drops that hedge.
3. **Group labels (30–38, chart and strip):** "38 **lighter drinkers**" and "11 **heavy drinkers**". C5's hedge is "overall": the groups were split at 3.2 L/day of **total** water (food, drinks and metabolism), and over half of it came from food (C13). The labels contradict the VO. The same slip is in the end card ("people who drank less") and the Instagram caption.

**Hedge nits (fix with the above):**
- "+40%" (34–38 chart and strip) lacks C6's "about".
- "+2 L a day" (12–15) and the VO "They added two liters a day" are unhedged. C2's quote is a "mean value of 2,000 mL … reference surplus", and `platform.json` already says "about 2 liters".
- C7/C11 should note that "drank" means total water intake.

**Rule miss:**
- The TikTok copy in `platform.json` lacks "Education, not medical advice." (channel rule: captions always carry it).

**To confirm:**
- The C13 row should state whether the 57.2 % food share was the baseline diet. If it was, the VO "Over half their water came from food", placed just after "Everyone got the extra water", is fine but could say "before the extra water".

**Not open:** Akdeniz 2018 is marked OPEN-unused. It is not used directly; the VO cites it only through S2's summary, as the claims notes say.

**Sound (not heard):**
- **Loudness:** −14.1 LUFS and TP −1.5 dBTP are fine. LRA is 1.8 LU, flatter than ep01's flagged 2.0.
- **Foley:** the skin-receipts water foley (`sr_pour`, `sr_drop`, `sr_swell`) is procedural band noise, the same issue noted on ep01.
- **SRT widows:** "it." (2), "day," (9), "study." (27), "weak." (29), "little." (34).
