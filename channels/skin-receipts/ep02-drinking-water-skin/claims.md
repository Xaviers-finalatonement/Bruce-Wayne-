# Claims — Skin Receipts Ep 2 "Does drinking water hydrate your skin?"

Status rule: **OK** only after the full source was opened and the line found. Search excerpt only = **OPEN**.
Checked by: Claude (full pages fetched via curl, text read, quote copied below) on 2026-11-07. Bruce may spot-check any line.

**Evidence verdict: UNKNOWN.** One small study (49 women, 4 weeks, no unsupplemented comparison group) found skin hydration readings rose after adding about 2 L of water a day, mostly in people who took in less water to begin with. A 2018 review of five such studies called the evidence weak; whether a person would notice is unclear.

Spoken hook: "Drinking water for skin? Forty-nine women tested it." (8 words.) Thread: what did the test find, how strong is it? Ending answers it: "Water might nudge skin hydration readings, mostly if you drink little. Proven? Not yet."
