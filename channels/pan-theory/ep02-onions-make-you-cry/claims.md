## Notes

- Peeled or not: the study's onions were peeled of the outermost dry brown skin ("The outermost dry layer (brown skin) was removed"), then cut into halves. The film shows peeled, pale halves under the rig and the line says "the thin, tough skin on each layer".
- The study's droplets came from a lab rig, not a person at a board; nothing in the script says droplets reach your eyes.
- Not used: primary enzyme/chemistry papers (C6/C7 are background stated in S1).
