### Factual and ethical items

- **F1 — OPEN (blocking): "it's the droplets" on the cover and in the YouTube title.**
  - **Where:** the `out/cover.png` tag "it's the DROPLETS" under "WHY ONIONS MAKE YOU CRY", and the `platform.json` YouTube title "Why onions make you cry (it's the droplets)".
  - **The problem:** both state that the droplets are why onions make you cry. The film itself says the opposite. The link is "suggested, not shown here" (C9, 40–44.5), and "the team tracked droplets, not tears" (C12, 58.7–61.4).
  - **Ledger:** no claims row covers it.
  - **Also loose:** the TikTok title "…A sharp knife helps" leaves the reader to infer "helps you cry less". Reword it to the supported claim: "a sharper blade made fewer droplets".
- **F2 — OPEN (blocking): "still a preprint" is probably no longer true.**
  - **Where:** the b13 VO (62.0–64.3), the sticker "one study · preprint" (64.0–64.9), the YouTube description ("this is one preprint"), the Instagram caption ("One preprint") and C13.
  - **What the search found:** a web search this round says the study is published in *PNAS* 122(42), e2512779122, in Oct 2025. The evidence is an APS March Meeting 2026 listing ([meetings-archive.aps.org/smt/2026/mar-l56/1](https://meetings-archive.aps.org/smt/2026/mar-l56/1/)) and a news report on the PNAS paper ([gujaratsamachar.com](https://english.gujaratsamachar.com/news/science-technology/scientists-find-way-to-prevent-crying-while-chopping-onions)).
  - **Not yet confirmed:** I have not opened the PNAS page. Under §5 it must be opened by a person.
  - **Why the ledger check falls short:** C13 rests on the arXiv page showing no journal reference. That is weak evidence, because arXiv records are often not updated.
  - **If it is confirmed:** drop "preprint" everywhere. Re-point S1 to the published version, and re-check every S1 quote and number against it (C1–C7, C9–C12). Numbers can change between a preprint and the journal version.
- **F3 — OPEN (minor, must close before a PASS): ledger coverage.**
  - **"maybe fewer tears":** the line (b14, 67.7–70.3) has no row of its own. It sits only in the C11 hedge column. Add a row with:
    - the claim, "a sharper knife: maybe fewer tears";
    - its basis, the authors' "widely held belief" / "experimental validation" framing plus Kohman's suggestion (C9);
    - its hedge, "maybe", with the note that no tears were measured.
  - **Any new wording:** add a row for any new cover or title wording.
  - **"The tough outer skin" (44.9):**
    - **The wording risk:** the line will be heard as the papery brown skin. S1's word is the epidermis, and the published summary speaks of the onion's "first layer".
    - **Check:** whether the rig's onions were peeled. The film shows unpeeled halves under the blade.
    - **Fix:** if they were peeled, show peeled halves and say "the thin, tough skin on each layer".
- **Clear:**
  - There is no diet, medical or product advice, and no brands.
  - "Food education, not diet advice." is on the end card.
  - The eye is shown without skin, so the skin-tone rule does not apply.
  - The 40 m/s → ≈ 90 mph conversion is right (89.5).
  - 20 ms = 1/50 s is right.
  - The "0.020 s" counter matches C5.
  - The "slow motion · frame NNNN" counter makes no factual claim.

### Verdict: **FAIL**

1. **Scores:** (a) = 3 and (h) = 3, where the gate needs ≥ 4. No score is below 3.
2. **Facts:** F1 and F2 are open and blocking. F3 is open.

### Defects, ranked

2. **F2:** "still a preprint" is probably false (PNAS, Oct 2025). It is in the VO, on screen, in two captions and in C13.
3. **(h) The subject itself:** the droplets are matte, grey and faceted, with stepped ghosts. The blade is a striated slab. The set has floating white rectangles.
4. **(a) The hook picture:** no visible eruption from the cut at 0.6–1.2 s. It is hazy and side-on.
5. **F3:** the "maybe fewer tears" row, plus the "outer skin" wording and the peeled-or-not check.
6. **Eye beat:** no tear on "Tears." (38.3). The "cornea" pointer ends on the pupil (35.5–39.9), so "cornea" can be misread as the black dot.
7. **End card (64.9–73.3):** near-blank cream, with no knife and no cut. It runs 8.4 s with almost no motion.
8. **Second-tier assets:** the cell world is green with paper shards, the b08 eye badge is flat 2D, and the cross-section is abstract.
9. **Captions:** the "up to" break (10.6) and the orphan "the" (44.9). Several stickers repeat the caption.
10. **Overhead:** the channel's overhead default is used only in b12.
