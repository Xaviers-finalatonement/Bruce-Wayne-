# Unreal, Actually — style sheet (draft name)

**Subject:** insane-but-true facts about space, time and mysteries: black holes, wormholes, the Bermuda Triangle, time running faster at your head.
**Promise:** facts that sound fake, then the real science, including where the myth ends.
**Look:** near-black with hard light and one hot colour. Big condensed type slams in. Each episode has a "case file" counter that ends on **TRUE**, **FALSE** or **UNPROVEN**.

## Palette

| Name | Hex | Use | Contrast |
|---|---|---|---|
| Abyss | `#08070D` | background | — |
| Static | `#F3F1FF` | text | 18.0:1 |
| Horizon | `#FF8A2A` | key word, TRUE stamp | 8.5:1 |
| Ultraviolet | `#A98BFF` | accent, UNPROVEN stamp | 7.5:1 |
| Haze | `#8D8AA6` | secondary text | 6.0:1 |

## Fonts

**Anton** 400 for big titles and stamps; **Barlow** 500/800 for captions. Both OFL and self-hosted.

## Camera

- Dramatic but motivated: a hard push toward the "impossible" thing, then a reveal pull-back that shows the scale.
- Light falling into a black hole leads the camera in.
- Myth vs science shown as a split frame.
- Shake is allowed only for impacts.

## Pacing and voice

- Fast hook, then room to breathe. A new beat every 1.5–2.5 s. About 160 words per minute.
- An amazed friend: "Wait, that's real?" Honest about theory vs evidence ("on paper, maybe; nobody's seen one").

## Sound

Deep hits, sub swells and glitchy risers.

## Signature

The **case file** stamp at the end: TRUE, FALSE or UNPROVEN.

## Never

Presenting myths or theories as fact, fake "leaked" footage, conspiracy framing.
