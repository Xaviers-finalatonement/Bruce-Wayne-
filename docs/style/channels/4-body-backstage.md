# Body Backstage — style sheet

**Subject:** the human body.
**Promise:** go backstage inside your own body, filmed like a premium medical-visualisation title.
**Look in one line:** a dark stage, translucent glowing tissue, rim light, shallow depth of field, one continuous journey.

## Fonts (self-hosted, OFL)

- **Instrument Serif** 400 for titles and structure names.
- **Manrope** 400/700 for captions and labels.

## Camera language

- Macro, steadicam-smooth travel through the body.
- Motivated by flow: the camera follows blood, a nerve signal or a breath.
- Rack focus to introduce each new structure. Slow push-ins.
- Scale descent in one continuous move: skin, then cell, then molecule.
- Anatomy is built from open anatomy references. Tissues are translucent with a subsurface glow. Never gory, never cartoon.

## Pacing

- Cinematic: beats of 2.5–3 s, one continuous journey per episode.
- Voice about 145 words per minute.

## Voice

- A calm, intimate documentary narrator: "Right now, while you watch this, your…".
- Respectful of every body. Hedges where the source does: animal or lab data is never "your body".

## Sound

A soft heartbeat bed, organic textures and airy tones.

## Captions and cover

- Captions: Manrope 700, Bone on Backstage, key word in Plasma or Nerve.
- Cover: dark, one glowing structure, a title in Instrument Serif.

## Signature

The **backstage pass**: open on an everyday outside moment (a yawn, a blush), dive through, and end back outside on the same moment.

## Never

Flat clay shapes, bright backgrounds, cartoon organs, gore, or medical advice.
