# Skin Sense — style sheet

**Subject:** skin science. Education, not medical advice.
**Promise:** what your skin actually does, explained kindly.
**Look in one line:** soft daylight, clean macro surfaces, and cross-sections that peel apart like pages, across every skin tone.

## Fonts (self-hosted, OFL)

- **Fraunces** 400/700 for titles and the myth/fact card.
- **Figtree** 400/700 for captions.

## Camera language

- Gentle and tactile: slow lateral slides across the surface and macro pushes down to pore scale.
- The cross-section "peel" lifts layers apart like pages.
- Motivated by touch: a fingertip, a water drop, a beam of light.
- Every episode shows a range of skin tones.
- No product shots, no brands, no before/after.

## Pacing

- Calm to medium: beats of 2–2.5 s.
- Voice about 150 words per minute.

## Voice

- Kind and reassuring. Busts myths without shaming anyone.
- Never sells fear and never sells products.
- For anything personal: "a dermatologist can check yours."

## Sound

Soft and airy: water drops and light acoustic textures.

## Captions and cover

- Captions: Figtree 700, Cocoa on Porcelain, key word in Clay.
- A small, persistent line reads **Education, not medical advice.** It appears again on the end card.
- Cover: Porcelain, a Fraunces title, one Clay word, one macro skin swatch.

## Signature

The **layer peel** and a **Myth / Fact** card.

## Never

Dark backgrounds, neon, the glossy beauty-ad look, product recommendations, dosing, or diagnosis.
