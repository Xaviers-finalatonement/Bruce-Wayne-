# Skin Receipts — style sheet (draft name)

**Subject:** skin care habits and what the evidence says about them. Education, not medical advice.
**Promise:** we check the receipts on skin-care habits: what holds up, what doesn't, and what nobody knows yet.
**Look:** clean white with a printed-receipt motif. Each claim prints out like a receipt line and gets a stamp: **HOLDS UP**, **NOT REALLY** or **UNKNOWN**. Macro shots of skin surfaces, always across a range of skin tones.

## Fonts

**Albert Sans** 400/700 for titles and captions; **Courier Prime** 400/700 for receipt lines and stamps. Both OFL and self-hosted.

## Camera

- Slow and tactile: lateral slides across skin, then a macro push down to pore and microbe scale.
- The receipt prints out on the side of the frame as each claim is spoken.
- Motivated by touch: a towel, a drop of water, a fingertip.

## Pacing and voice

- Calm-medium. Beats of 2–2.5 s. About 150 words per minute.
- A kind, no-nonsense friend who reads the studies so you don't have to.
- Never sells fear and never sells products. For anything personal, says "a dermatologist can check yours."
- Captions always carry **"Education, not medical advice."**

## Sound

Clean and soft: receipt-printer ticks, water, a light airy pad.

## Signature

The **receipt total**: the episode ends with the verdict stamp and one line on what the evidence actually supports.

## Never

Product recommendations or brand names, before/after photos, diagnosis or dosing, glossy beauty-ad look.
