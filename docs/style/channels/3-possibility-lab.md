# Possibility Lab — style sheet

**Subject:** "what if" questions.
**Promise:** ask the impossible question, then follow the real science to where it ends.
**Look in one line:** a bold violet test chamber with matte clay-like shapes, colour-blocked panels and a trial counter.

## Palette

| Name | Hex | Use | Contrast |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lab Violet | `#4B22D6` | background | — |
| Cream | `#FFF6E5` | text | 7.8:1 on Lab Violet |
| Lime | `#C8F135` | the "what if", key word | 6.4:1 on Lab Violet |
| Coral | `#FF6A55` | consequence or danger panels | Charcoal on Coral 6.1:1 |
| Charcoal | `#1E1A26` | text on Cream, Lime or Coral panels | 13.1:1 on Lime, 15.9:1 on Cream |

## Fonts (self-hosted, OFL)

- **Bricolage Grotesque** 600/800 for titles and the verdict card.
- **DM Sans** 400/700 for captions.

## Camera language

- Energetic but always motivated: a snap zoom onto the consequence and a whip-pan on "what if".
- Scale jumps: cut from a person to a city to a planet as the effect grows.
- Split screen: **Normal** next to **What if**.
- Shake only when something physically hits.
- Shapes are matte and soft-shadowed, like clay models in a studio. Never photoreal.

## Pacing

- The fastest channel. A new beat every 1.5–2 s.
- Escalates in three steps, for example the first second, the first day, the first year.
- Voice about 165 words per minute.

## Voice

- A curious, excited friend: "Okay, but what if…", "here's where it gets weird."
- Honest about guesses: "nobody has tried this, but the maths says…".

## Sound

Playful percussion, pops and lab beeps, with risers under each escalation step.

## Captions and cover

- Captions: DM Sans 700, Cream on Lab Violet, key word in Lime.
- Cover: Lab Violet, a small "WHAT IF" tag, and the question in big Bricolage Grotesque, partly on a Lime panel.

## Signature

A **Trial No.** counter, the three-step escalation timeline, and a closing **Verdict** card.

## Never

Realistic space, medical realism, paper textures, serif fonts.
