# Reality Rulebook — style sheet

**Subject:** physics and how reality works.
**Promise:** the rules reality runs on, shown like a rulebook you can test at home.
**Look in one line:** an annotated rulebook come to life: warm paper, ink-line diagrams, one red rule mark.

## Palette

| Name | Hex | Use | Contrast |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paper | `#F3EFE6` | background | — |
| Ink | `#15171C` | text, lines, objects | 15.6:1 on Paper |
| Rule Red | `#B8302A` | the one rule, the key word | 5.2:1 on Paper |
| Graphite | `#555C68` | secondary labels | 5.9:1 on Paper |
| Marker | `#F2C230` | highlighter behind Ink text only | Ink on Marker 10.7:1 |
| Grid | `#BFD0E0` | graph-paper grid, never text | — |

## Fonts (self-hosted, OFL)

- **Space Grotesk** 500/700 for titles and captions.
- **JetBrains Mono** 400/700 for numbers, units, labels and the "Rule No." stamp.

## Camera language

- Orthographic or long-lens, close to flat, like a diagram you can walk into.
- Locked-off by default. It moves only in straight lines along x, y or z, with an eased start and stop.
- Every move has a reason: an arrow points and the camera follows; "look closer" pushes in.
- No orbiting, no handheld shake, no floating drone moves.
- Objects are drawn as ink outlines with flat fills (toon shading). Never glossy 3D.

## Pacing

- Measured. A new visual idea every 2–3 s.
- Hold 1 s of stillness on the moment the rule is stated.
- Voice about 150 words per minute.

## Voice

- Dry, precise and quietly funny: a physics-teacher friend who is off duty.
- Short sentences, and the plain word first. "Here's the rule."
- Ends with the rule in one line and a "try it yourself" moment from your day.

## Sound

Dry foley (pencil scratch, paper slide, clicks) with a sparse, steady pulse. No big cinematic swells.

## Captions and cover

- Captions: Space Grotesk 700, Ink on Paper, key word in Rule Red; mono for any number.
- Cover: Paper, a 3–5 word Ink headline, a red underline drawn under the key word, and one line diagram.

## Signature

Each episode stamps one **Rule No. _n_** in mono, with a red underline drawn on as the voice says it.

## Never

Black space backgrounds, glow, neon, serif fonts, or photoreal renders. Those belong to other channels.
