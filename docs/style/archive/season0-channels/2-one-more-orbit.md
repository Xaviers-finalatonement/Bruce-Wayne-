# One More Orbit — style sheet

**Subject:** space.
**Promise:** space at its real scale, one more lap around something astonishing.
**Look in one line:** true-black void, bodies lit by a single sun, thin orbit lines, slow heavy camera.

## Palette

| Name | Hex | Use | Contrast |
|---|---|---|---|
| Void | `#04060C` | background | — |
| Deep | `#0D1630` | gradients, panels | Starlight on Deep 15.4:1 |
| Starlight | `#E9EEFF` | text | 17.5:1 on Void |
| Sun | `#FFB547` | the key word, the light source | 11.5:1 on Void |
| Ion | `#59DDE3` | orbit lines, data | 12.4:1 on Void |
| Dust | `#8E97B5` | secondary text | 7.0:1 on Void |

## Fonts (self-hosted, OFL)

- **Unbounded** 500/700 for titles and big on-screen numbers.
- **Atkinson Hyperlegible** 400/700 for captions.

## Camera language

- The camera has mass: long dollies, wide arcs, and easing over 2 s or more.
- Moves are motivated by orbits (the camera rides an orbit path) or by scale (the slow zoom-out that shows how big).
- No cuts in the middle of a move. No shake.
- One light source, physically placed. No lens-flare spam, no fake nebula wallpaper.

## Pacing

- The slowest channel. Holds of 3–4 s, with a breath before each reveal.
- Silence is allowed.
- Voice about 140 words per minute.

## Voice

- Warm, quiet and awed: a late-night talk under the sky.
- Speaks to "you". Turns size into time and travel you know ("if you drove there…", "a flight from LA to New York").

## Sound

Low drones and soft sub swells as score only. Space itself makes no sound, so no whooshes on objects.

## Captions and cover

- Captions: Atkinson Hyperlegible 700, Starlight on Void, key word in Sun.
- Cover: black, the lit edge of one body, an Unbounded title, one Sun-coloured word.

## Signature

The **one more orbit** ending: the camera finishes one last loop and pulls back to Earth, or to you.

## Never

Paper textures, cartoon outlines, cream or bright backgrounds, serif titles.
