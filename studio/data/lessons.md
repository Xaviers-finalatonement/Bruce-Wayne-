# Lessons

Small notes on how the studio works. Add your own below.

## The posting rhythm

- Check a film in **Review** first. Approve the video, then approve the text, for each platform.
- Approving never posts anything. It only puts the item in the **Upload** queue.
- Post by hand, then press **Mark as posted** so the calendar stays true.

## Posting from a phone

1. Download the video and the thumbnail to your phone first.
2. Copy the title or caption, then the hashtags, one at a time.
3. Open the upload page and paste.

Instagram: post from the Instagram app on your phone. The web page cannot upload reels easily.

## Words and facts

- Plain friendly words. Short sentences.
- Every claim comes from the episode's claims list. Add no new facts when editing text.
- Skin Receipts text always ends with "Education, not medical advice."
- Instagram captions start with the channel name, because three channels share one account.
